we need you (yes, you)

Come play dress‑up with us

Whimsy Works runs entirely on volunteers. Performers, costume makers, photographers, planners: there's a spot for you, and no minimum commitment.

Get involved

why people join

Our volunteers are the whole show

We bring theme-park quality character visits to pediatric hospitals, fundraisers, and community events, and every one of them is put on by volunteers. You'll be bringing joy to kids who need it most, including kids in hospital and in communities that don't often get visits like this.

You'll do it alongside a supportive team of people who love characters and giving back as much as you do. You'll pick up real experience in performance, character work, event coordination, and running a nonprofit. And you volunteer on your own time: we work around your availability and never require a minimum commitment.

ways to help

What we're looking for

Performers

Bring princesses, superheroes, and other beloved characters to life with authenticity and warmth.

Costume & makeup artists

Make the magic behind the scenes: costume maintenance, wig styling, and character makeup.

Event support

Help with event logistics, photography, and social media, and keep the day running smoothly.

Behind-the-scenes admin

Scheduling, communications, fundraising, and the organizing that keeps everything else possible.

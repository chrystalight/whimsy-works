how it started

A handful of cosplayers at a children's book bank.

That's where Whimsy Works began: a few of us in costume at the Toronto Children's Book Bank. It has since grown into a province-wide collective of artists and volunteers.

We handmake and maintain dozens of size-inclusive costumes ourselves, and we work with charities, non-profits, and families across Ontario. Nobody here is paid. We do it because we've seen what happens when a princess walks into a hospital room.

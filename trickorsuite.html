What is Trick or Suite?

Trick or Suite is Starlight Children's Foundation Canada's accessible Halloween celebration for seriously ill children and their families. More than 25 hotel suites are transformed into a safe, fully accessible wonderland, where every room becomes its own unique trick-or-treat station filled with costumes, candy, toy giveaways, and immersive fun. Whimsy Works is proud to join the celebration, bringing beloved princesses, superheroes, and characters to life throughout the afternoon.

Volunteer FAQ

I'm new to princessing — can I still volunteer?

Yes! This is a great event for newer volunteers. There's lots of support staff from Starlight, plus plenty of experienced princesses around to help you out.

I'm taking transit to the event — can I change on site?

Yes. We'll have a Whimsy Works break room and changing areas on site, and all costuming will be brought up in advance.

Can I bring a handler or guest?

Yes, but we need to keep it to a maximum of one handler per cosplayer. Instructions to register your handler will come soon.

Will there be food, snacks, and drinks?

Yes! Register your dietary restrictions in the form. We also encourage you to bring a reusable water bottle.

Can I help with decor planning even if I can't volunteer on the day?

Yes! Fill out the form, skip all the sections about the characters, and we'll be in touch.

Do I need a Vulnerable Sector Check?

Yes, and it must be less than 5 years old. We can initiate one for you if you need it, but we have to get that started as soon as possible.

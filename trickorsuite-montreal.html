Trick or Suite Montreal

October 24th · Montreal

Register Your Interest

What is Trick or Suite?

Trick or Suite is one of the accessible events put on by Starlight Children's Foundation, a Canadian charity that creates joyful, inclusive experiences for kids facing serious and life-altering medical challenges across the country. For Trick or Suite, Starlight takes over a hotel and decorates all the rooms, so kids can trick-or-treat up and down the halls in a fully accessible and medically safe environment.

Starlight's Montreal team is looking for princess performers and experienced cosplayers in the Montreal area to bring the magic to life at their October 24th event — and asked us to help spread the word!

How This Works

A friendly heads-up so you know exactly what you're signing up for:

  • Whimsy Works is not coordinating this event. We coordinate a group of princess volunteers for Starlight's Toronto Trick or Suite, and their Montreal staff asked us to connect interested performers to them. We're simply helping make the introduction!
  • You'll be volunteering directly with Starlight Children's Foundation. Once you register your interest, Starlight's Montreal team takes it from there for the details of the day.
  • We can help coordinate your Vulnerable Sector Check. If you need one, let us know in the form and we can get that started — but we have to begin as soon as possible.
  • Costuming is not provided. This is a great fit for experienced princess performers and cosplayers who already have their own costumes ready to go.

Volunteer FAQ

Who is this event for?

We're looking to connect experienced princess performers and cosplayers in the Montreal area directly with Starlight Children's Foundation. If you already have your own costume and love bringing characters to life for kids, this is for you!

Is Whimsy Works running this event?

No. Whimsy Works coordinates princess volunteers for Starlight's Toronto Trick or Suite, and their Montreal team asked us to reach out to Montreal performers. You'll be volunteering directly with Starlight.

Will costuming be provided?

No — please bring your own costume. This event is best suited to experienced performers and cosplayers who are set up with their own costuming.

Do I need a Vulnerable Sector Check?

Yes, and it must be less than 5 years old. We can help coordinate one for you if you need it, but we have to get that started as soon as possible — so let us know in the form.

What is the event environment like?

Starlight takes over a hotel and decorates all the rooms, so kids can trick-or-treat up and down the halls in a fully accessible and medically safe environment. It's a warm, welcoming space designed around the kids' needs.

Who do I contact with questions?

Feel free to email us at info@whimsyworks.ca and we'll help point you in the right direction.
